Figure 3 | Displayed objective trajectories and signed updates for illustrative practice data. (a) Current objective values for Proposed (blue circles), Adaptive (teal squares), and Baseline (slate triangles) over ten displayed iterations; lower objective values are better. The open marker indicates the direction-aware running best at the single Proposed point where it differs from the current value. (b) Signed adjacent-step change, defined as Δobjective = objective[t] − objective[t−1]; negative values indicate a decrease under minimization. The dataset contains one deterministic trajectory per algorithm, so no uncertainty bands or statistical comparisons are shown. The data are illustrative practice data and should not be interpreted as a real experimental result.
